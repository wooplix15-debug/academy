# Version 0.2 review and changes
The first version supplied a strong course structure but most lessons were outlines. Several practical checks lacked reproducible input and trainer answers. The migration module required 50 rows while its starter fixture had only eight. Capstone work was not clearly accumulated through the course. Corporate follow-through wording could imply extra hours in the final module.

This revision adds 36 detailed learner lessons and separate trainer keys, 72 module-linked questions, a 50-row migration fixture, executable local exercises, 9 CSV worksheets, a portfolio workbook and a 20-case agent evaluation matrix. Every lesson includes demonstration input, expected behavior, failure and recovery tests, a different independent task and timed teaching sequence. Earlier modules contribute to the final portfolio. Four course offers and their 204 combined hours are preserved; CAW follow-through is four hours across the program, not four new hours in the final module.

The Word and PDF teaching guide explain the course progression, visuals, worked examples and operations. Full module instructions are in materials/lesson_plans and the offline learning studio. Course content remains editable and requires technical rehearsal before enrollment.

The optional content agent now receives deeper teaching requirements. Its deterministic wrapper tests and local practice tests do not establish live-model quality or connected Zoho behavior. The 20-case matrix is supplied for actual evaluation; no actual model results are prefilled.

Use team/practical_content_register.csv to assign author, technical reviewer and teaching reviewer. Keep a versioned change log. Read practical lessons aloud, rehearse every live step in the selected product edition, replace synthetic examples with sanitized reviewed experience, then record approval. Rebuild the teaching guide and studio after editing source files, so they do not drift.

The operations academic policy is aligned with the new practical rubric. The earlier short worked examples and fixtures are supplemental; the primary version 0.2 fixtures and question bank are identified in README.md.
