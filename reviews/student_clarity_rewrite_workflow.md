# Student Clarity Rewrite Workflow

This is the editing approach for the current student books. It preserves course scope while making the writing easier to read and practise.

## What to change

- Convert long policy-style paragraphs into short explanations with an example.
- Turn technical comparisons into tables.
- Give every practice task supplied inputs, steps, expected results and a self-check.
- Keep one warning where it is needed; remove repeated warnings elsewhere.
- Use a visual only when it makes a handoff, decision or system boundary easier to understand.

## What to keep

- Approved course title and chapter order.
- Case records, source IDs, versions and fictional-company boundaries.
- Permission checks, human approval, evidence requirements and recovery behavior.
- Existing exercise data and student solutions unless they are internally inconsistent.
- Official reference links, with a date and environment check before delivery.

## Editing order

1. **Course 3 — AI & Agentic AI Builder:** rewrite Chapters 1–20. Start with Chapters 2, 7, 10, 11 and 12 because they carry the most dense conceptual and product-specific writing.
2. **Course 2 — Developer & Implementation Engineer:** rewrite Chapters 11–13 and 20 first; keep request/response fixtures and make build steps explicit.
3. **Course 1 — Business Process & Corporate Automation:** shorten Chapters 2–8 and move deep reference material into optional reference sections.
4. **Course 4 and Course 5:** lighter pass. Their chapter structure is already stronger; focus on direct language and completed business artifacts.

## Definition of done for one chapter

- A new learner can state the business problem in their own words.
- Every technical term is defined or linked to the glossary.
- The worked case supplies all facts needed for its conclusion.
- The guided practice can be completed without hidden inputs.
- The independent challenge changes the situation enough to test understanding.
- The answer explains why, not only what.
- Product-dependent steps are marked for environment verification.
- The rendered page has readable headings, one study guide, valid links and no raw Markdown.
