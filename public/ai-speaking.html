Choose the right session format

01

Talk and discussion

Introduce a topic, work through relevant examples and leave time for questions. Suitable when the audience needs a shared understanding.

02

Technical session

Examine a system design or implementation example in more detail. Agree the audience's technical background before choosing the scope.

03

Practical workshop

Work on an exercise or a business opportunity with participants. Agree the tools, access, group size and expected outputs in advance.

What makes a session useful

The material should explain technical ideas in language the audience understands. A worked example needs to show the original problem, the proposed approach and the checks that determine whether the result is useful.

Demonstrations depend on the tools and access agreed for the event. Future developments can be discussed as possibilities; predictions and proposed time savings should not be presented as measured results.

Information to include in an event enquiry

  1. 01

    The organisation, event purpose and audience, including their experience with AI.

  2. 02

    The date, location or online format, time zone and expected audience size.

  3. 03

    The preferred topic, session format and the question or skill the audience should take away.

  4. 04

    Any practical constraints, including tools, internet access and requirements for a demonstration.

Availability, topic, scope, fees and arrangements are agreed before the event is confirmed.
