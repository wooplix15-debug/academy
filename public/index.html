GOOD TO KNOW

Before you
get started.

Are these official Zoho certification courses?+

These are Wooplix Academy training programmes. They do not promise or imply an official Zoho certification.

Are dates, fees and delivery details fixed?+

They are confirmed for each cohort or team proposal. Contact Wooplix to discuss current availability and scope.

Will the course guarantee a job or client project?+

No. The programmes focus on practical learning and reviewed work; employment and client assignments are not guaranteed.

Can a course be tailored for our team?+

Yes. Team training can be scoped around learner roles, current tools and an agreed process or use case.
