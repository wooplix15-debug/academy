WHO IT’S FOR

Zoho implementers and consultants who want structured practice across a client-style delivery cycle.

WHAT TO BRING

Working familiarity with Zoho applications and business processes is recommended. Final entry requirements are set for each cohort.

COURSE PROJECT

A simulated client engagement in which learners present a documented implementation plan and review its risks and acceptance checks.

What you’ll
learn and practise.

Outline status

This practicum outline is a proposed structure for Wooplix trainer review. Confirm the final module sequence and assessments before promoting it as a fixed syllabus.

01Discovery and stakeholder interviews+

Clarify the business outcome, users, current process, pain points and open decisions..

Practice: Run a simulated discovery interview and separate verified facts from assumptions..

02Requirements, scope and acceptance+

Turn stakeholder needs into prioritized requirements, boundaries and testable acceptance criteria..

Practice: Prepare a scope brief, assumptions log and change-control questions..

03Solution options and design+

Compare supported Zoho configuration, integration and custom development options against the requirements..

Practice: Create a system map, record-ownership notes and a solution decision log..

04Delivery plan, roles and risks+

Plan work, responsibilities, dependencies, access and client decisions before implementation begins..

Practice: Build a delivery plan and risk register for the simulated engagement..

05Testing, change and client review+

Plan user acceptance, defect triage, adoption support and release decisions..

Practice: Review test evidence, prioritize issues and prepare a client walkthrough..

06Documentation and handover+

Explain the solution, limitations, operating steps and support responsibilities clearly..

Practice: Present the implementation pack and respond to client questions..
