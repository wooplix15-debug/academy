01Consulting Role and Sales-to-Delivery Handoff+

Topics: Client context, outcomes, proposals, commitments, assumptions, discovery gaps and commercial boundaries.

Practice: Review the handoff and produce an engagement brief.

02Stakeholder Discovery and Kickoff+

Topics: Interviews, facilitation, conflicting needs, decision rights, stakeholders, dependencies, early sizing and objectives.

Practice: Produce discovery notes, charter and initial estimate assumptions.

03Process Analysis and Redesign+

Topics: Swimlanes, exceptions, cancellation/rescheduling, handoffs, bottlenecks, operational controls and improvement measures.

Practice: Validate current-state and future-state models.

04Requirements and Traceability+

Topics: Requirements, user stories, non-functional needs, acceptance criteria, priorities, fit-gap analysis and requirement-to-test links.

Practice: Build a traceable requirements register.

05Scope and Statement of Work+

Topics: Deliverables, assumptions, exclusions, responsibilities, dependencies, acceptance terms, initial estimates and change mechanisms.

Practice: Draft a statement of work and scope baseline.

06Solution Architecture and Product Fit+

Topics: Application selection, native versus custom design, record ownership, integration boundaries, edition constraints and architecture decisions.

Practice: Prepare an architecture and fit-gap pack.

07Access, Identity and Environments+

Topics: Roles, profiles, sharing, connection identities, test environments, sensitive data, audit needs and environment readiness.

Practice: Produce an access matrix and environment plan.

08Data Migration Strategy+

Topics: Assessment, cleansing ownership, mapping, identifiers, import order, trial migrations, rejected records, delta changes and reconciliation.

Practice: Prepare a migration workbook and rehearsal evidence.

09Delivery Planning and Estimation+

Topics: Work breakdown, dependencies, capacity, configuration/development effort, testing, migration, training, contingency and estimate refinement.

Practice: Defend a delivery plan and estimate.

10Prototyping and Design Validation+

Topics: Small prototypes covering the full process, demonstrations, feedback, decisions, requirement refinement and difficult assumptions.

Practice: Present a working prototype and reviewed decisions.

11Integration and Quality Management+

Topics: Interface ownership, mappings, duplicate/retry behaviour, test strategy, defects, severity, root cause and retesting.

Practice: Produce integration specifications and test evidence.

12Scope Change and Communication+

Topics: Impact analysis, prioritisation, approvals, conflicting requests, risks/issues/assumptions/dependencies and status reporting.

Practice: Handle a change request and steering review.

13User Acceptance Testing+

Topics: Business-led scenarios, representative roles, expected results, traceability, defect decisions and acceptance evidence.

Practice: Facilitate UAT and record acceptance decisions.

14Cutover, Release and Recovery+

Topics: Readiness, configuration promotion, final data changes, sequencing, communications, go/no-go decisions, smoke tests and recovery.

Practice: Run a go-live simulation with a cutover runbook.

15Training, Adoption and Support Handover+

Topics: Training for each user role, administrator guides, procedures, ownership transfer, triage, escalation and support boundaries.

Practice: Deliver training and support handover packs.

16Value Review, Closure and Client Presentation+

Topics: Outcomes versus baseline, adoption evidence, commitments, improvements, commercial closure, retrospective and executive presentation.

Practice: Present the final client pack and defend individual work.

FINAL PROJECT

Evergreen Field Services Implementation

Act as the implementation team for a fictional client needing service intake, customer visibility, dispatch coordination, financial handoffs and management reporting.

What you will produce

  • Discovery notes, stakeholder map and project charter.
  • Statement of work, scope baseline, assumptions and estimate.
  • Process maps and traceable requirements.
  • Architecture, access design and environment plan.
  • Migration workbook and rehearsal results.
  • Working end-to-end prototype.
  • Integration specifications, tests, defects and UAT evidence.
  • Handled scope-change and delivery-risk scenarios.
  • Cutover/recovery runbook.
  • Training materials and support handover.
  • Outcome review, improvement backlog and client presentation.
  • Each learner explains their contribution and responds to a client-style change, migration or incident scenario.

How learning is assessed

Present the client project pack and explain your contribution. Review covers discovery, product fit, requirement-to-test links, delivery decisions, scope and client communication. Respond to a client-style change, migration or incident scenario.
