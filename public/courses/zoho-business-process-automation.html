Zoho Business Process & Corporate Automation

WHO IT’S FOR

Operations, sales, finance, HR and service teams; business owners and Zoho administrators.

WHAT TO BRING

No coding required. Basic spreadsheet and web application skills are useful.

COURSE PROJECT

01Business Process Discovery+

Topics: Process triggers, participants, decisions, handoffs, exceptions, bottlenecks, rework, mapping the current process and proposed changes, starting measurements and acceptance criteria.

Practice: Map a business process and define its measurable improvement goal.

02Zoho Ecosystem and Solution Selection+

Topics: Roles of CRM, Books, Desk, People, Flow, Creator and Analytics; Zoho One administration; application selection, data ownership and integration choices.

Practice: Create an application map identifying the system that owns each record.

03Organisation and User Administration+

Topics: Organisation settings, locale, currencies, business hours, holidays, users, teams, identity controls and user onboarding/offboarding.

Practice: Configure a training organisation and user-lifecycle checklist.

04CRM Data Architecture+

Topics: Leads, Contacts, Accounts, Deals and activities; custom modules, fields, lookups, layouts, subforms, validation and data dictionaries.

Practice: Build a CRM data model and field dictionary.

05Roles, Profiles and Record Access+

Topics: Role hierarchy, profiles, field restrictions, sharing, ownership, teams, territories and audit visibility.

Practice: Demonstrate permitted and denied actions for representative users.

06Data Preparation and Migration+

Topics: Data profiling, cleansing, field mapping, identifiers, duplicates, relationship-preserving import order, rejected records, reconciliation and backup planning.

Practice: Import a deliberately messy dataset and reconcile the result.

07Lead Capture and Customer Engagement+

Topics: Webforms and Forms handoffs, lead assignment, conversion, email templates, activities, follow-ups, communication preferences and tracking where enquiries come from.

Practice: Build an enquiry-to-qualified-lead workflow.

08Sales Operations and Management+

Topics: Pipelines, stage definitions, scoring, segmentation, territories, forecasts, targets, views, macros and exception reporting.

Practice: Configure sales-management views and operating rules.

09Workflow Automation+

Topics: Triggers, criteria, immediate and scheduled actions, notifications, tasks, field updates, execution order, repeated execution and automation conflicts.

Practice: Build and test an automation set with exception cases.

10Blueprint, Approvals and Journey Orchestration+

Topics: States, transitions, entry/exit criteria, mandatory information, approval and review processes, escalations and supported CommandCenter scenarios.

Practice: Implement a controlled process with approval and exception routes.

11Guided Screens and User Actions+

Topics: Canvas, layouts, wizards, Kiosk Studio, screens for specific tasks, guided actions, usability and accessibility.

Practice: Create a guided sales or service task.

12Products, Pricing and Commercial Records+

Topics: Products, price books, quote line items, discounts, approval controls, configure-price-quote concepts and relationships among commercial records.

Practice: Demonstrate a quotation with pricing and approval rules.

13Books and the Sales-to-Finance Handoff+

Topics: Customers, contact persons, items, quotes, orders, invoices, payments, receivables, adjustments, tax/currency settings, mappings, synchronisation and reconciliation.

Practice: Trace a quote-to-payment scenario and investigate a discrepancy.

14Cross-Application Automation with Flow+

Topics: Event, polling, scheduled, email and webhook triggers; actions, conditions, branching, mapping, connections, execution history and failed-run handling.

Practice: Build a cross-app flow and recover a failed handoff.

15Customer Service with Desk+

Topics: Ticket intake, departments, queues, assignment, service-level targets, escalations, notifications, CRM context, knowledge-base ownership and service reporting.

Practice: Configure ticket routing and escalation; publish a support article.

16Employee Processes with People+

Topics: Employee records, onboarding, leave, attendance, shifts, timesheets, documents, approvals, employee cases and confidential access.

Practice: Build an onboarding or leave-approval process.

17Reporting and Zoho Analytics+

Topics: Reports, connectors, relationships, data grain, formulas, pivots, dashboards, filters, sharing, refresh, duplicated totals after joins and metric definitions.

Practice: Build a dashboard and reconcile it to source records.

18Testing, Rollout and Administration+

Topics: Functional and access testing, user acceptance, release readiness, configuration records, training, support routing and controlled improvements.

Practice: Prepare a launch pack, user guide and improvement backlog.

FINAL PROJECT

Meridian Supply

Configure a connected enquiry-to-order and customer-service process for a fictional business.

What you will produce

  • Current-state and future-state process maps.
  • Application map, data dictionary and access matrix.
  • CRM pipeline, lead capture, assignment and controlled transitions.
  • Follow-up automation and one guided user experience.
  • Books handoff with verified mappings.
  • Flow integration and Desk service process.
  • Separate People operational exercise.
  • Dashboard reconciled to source records.
  • Demonstration of a duplicate, denied action and failed handoff.
  • User acceptance evidence, user instructions and support ownership.

How learning is assessed

Show your configured process and submit the project files. Review covers business fit, configuration, data quality, access, exception handling and user adoption. Each learner explains their own work and demonstrates a duplicate, denied action or failed handoff.
