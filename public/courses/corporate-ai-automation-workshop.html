WHO IT’S FOR

Department leaders and employee teams bringing one process they want to improve.

WHAT TO BRING

A sponsor identifies the process, participants and available tools. Use synthetic examples during exercises.

COURSE PROJECT

A prioritized opportunity and a reviewed pilot plan for one selected process.

COURSE SYLLABUS

What you’ll
learn and practise.

Open a module to see its focus and practical work. Delivery dates, fees and account access are confirmed with Wooplix for each cohort.

01Business baseline+

map a current departmental process; identify measurable delays.

Topics: Process walkthrough; Role mapping; Baseline measures.

Practice: Map lead follow up, support triage or employee onboarding.

02AI assisted work+

test an assisted task; identify where review is required.

Topics: Prompt examples; Output verification; Sensitive data handling.

Practice: Use synthetic inputs to draft a follow up or support summary.

03Automation opportunity design+

prioritize feasible opportunities; explain exception handling.

Topics: Value and feasibility; Workflow design; Tool and permission needs.

Practice: Rank three opportunities and diagram one small pilot.

04Pilot and follow through+

define pilot acceptance and ownership; prepare a discovery handoff.

Topics: Pilot scope; Success measures; Next decision.

Practice: Finalize a two week pilot brief in this module's one independent hour. The program has four total independent hours distributed across its four modules.

PRACTICAL WORK

Finish with work
you can explain.

A prioritized opportunity and a reviewed pilot plan for one selected process.

Project evidence

Current-state process and baseline, opportunity scorecard, sample assisted output, workflow sketch and pilot brief with an owner and acceptance measures.

How learning is reviewed

Review of the process baseline, opportunity scorecard and pilot brief. The workshop is not a practitioner credential.
