01AI and Automation Literacy+

Topics: Rules, integrations, generative assistance and agents; capabilities, limitations and business demonstrations.

Practice: Classify sample business tasks as rules, integrations, generative assistance or agents; note one limitation for each.

02Process Discovery and Problem Framing+

Topics: Triggers, people involved, handoffs, decisions, queues, rework, exceptions and customer impact; facts versus assumptions.

Practice: Map one familiar process, including triggers, roles, handoffs and exceptions; mark facts separately from assumptions.

03Opportunity Identification+

Topics: Sales, service, finance, HR and operations examples; document handling, knowledge retrieval, decision support and cross-app work.

Practice: Turn recurring issues into use-case cards showing the affected team, task, trigger and expected result.

04Data and Organisational Readiness+

Topics: Source quality, ownership, access, integrations, licences, process stability, support and stakeholder readiness.

Practice: Score one process for data ownership, access, integration, licences, process stability and support readiness.

05Value, Costs and Business Case+

Topics: Volumes, effort, errors, rework, implementation costs, licences, model usage, human review and support; time saved compared with actual cost savings.

Practice: Estimate volume, staff effort, errors and rework; separate released capacity from cash savings.

06Prioritisation and Solution Selection+

Topics: Value, feasibility, complexity, data readiness, failure consequences, reversibility and ownership; native features, Flow, development and AI options.

Practice: Compare native product features, Flow, custom development and AI against value, feasibility and risk.

07Human Responsibility and Operating Controls+

Topics: Decision ownership, review points, customer/employee impact, data handling, escalation and incident responsibility.

Practice: Create a responsibility map for data access, human review, decision ownership, escalation and incident handling.

08Pilot Design and Measurement+

Topics: Audience, process boundary, baseline comparison, representative cases, quality targets, adoption measures and success/stop criteria.

Practice: Draft a pilot charter with a clear boundary, baseline, representative cases, measures and stop criteria.

09Adoption and Change Management+

Topics: Affected roles, communication, user practice, feedback, process updates, resistance and accessibility.

Practice: Prepare a change plan for affected roles, practice, communications, feedback and process updates.

10Leadership Review and Next Steps+

Topics: Evidence, assumptions, costs, dependencies, unresolved questions and proceed/revise/defer decisions.

Practice: Present the evidence, assumptions, costs and open questions, then record a proceed, revise or defer decision.

FINAL PROJECT

Business Automation Pilot Proposal

Select one process and submit:

What you will produce

  • One-page executive proposal.
  • Current-state and future-state process maps.
  • Evidence-backed baseline and assumptions register.
  • Comparison of plausible solution options.
  • Value/cost model and prioritisation rationale.
  • Data, integration and ownership requirements.
  • Pilot boundaries, test cases, success measures and stop criteria.
  • Adoption plan and leadership decision.

How learning is assessed

Present the pilot proposal and explain the evidence, assumptions and choice of solution. Review covers data quality, realistic costs and value, measurable results and clear ownership. The proposal must include success measures and stop criteria.
