How the learning material is organised

Each chapter explains the topic before asking you to apply it. Worked cases show the inputs and reasoning. Exercises let you practise the method, and the solutions help you check your understanding.

The technical programme includes business examples with access rules, approval steps and failure cases. The corporate workshop focuses on selecting and planning a sensible pilot. The Zoho programmes develop the platform and implementation skills needed around that work.
