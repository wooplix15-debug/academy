AI CONSULTING

Decide where AI belongs in your business.

Bring a process, a recurring problem or an implementation idea. We will examine the current work, compare the options and identify a practical first step.

Choose a consulting session

For leaders and delivery teams

A useful starting point is one business question: which task needs to improve, who owns it and how will you know that the change helped?

What we can work on

01

Choosing an AI opportunity

Review repetitive work, information gaps and slow decisions. Compare AI with simpler alternatives and identify a problem worth addressing.

02

Planning implementation

Assess the available data, existing systems, permissions and team skills. Define a pilot with a clear boundary and a way to measure its usefulness.

03

Preparing the team

Identify where people need to review outputs, approve actions and handle exceptions. Plan training, ownership and support alongside the technology.

How a consulting engagement works

  1. 01

    Understand the process. Walk through the current work, the people involved and the problem that needs attention.

  2. 02

    Compare possible approaches. Consider existing tools, rules, automation and AI, including the dependencies and limitations of each option.

  3. 03

    Define a pilot. Agree its scope, required access, measures, human review and the conditions for continuing or stopping.

  4. 04

    Plan the next stage. Identify implementation work, responsibilities, training and support. A larger engagement has its own agreed scope and deliverables.

What to bring to the first conversation

Share a short description of your business, the process you want to improve, the systems involved and the decision you need help making. Include your time zone and any important deadline. A brief example is useful; confidential client documents are not needed for the initial enquiry.
