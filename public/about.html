Start with the work

A useful AI solution starts with understanding the people, information and decisions involved. The technology should support that work and give the team a clear way to judge whether it helps.

How I approach a business problem

I start by understanding the current process, including its delays, exceptions and responsibilities. That makes it easier to decide whether the answer is better software, a straightforward automation, AI assistance or an agent that uses connected tools.

The next step is to define a small piece of work that can be evaluated. The people involved need to know what the system may do, what it must ask permission to do and how an incorrect result will be handled. A pilot should help the business make a decision about the next investment.
