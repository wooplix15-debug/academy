# Content Quality Checklist

Confirm the audience and observable outcomes. Sum facilitated and independent hours. Preserve prerequisite order. Map each assignment and question to a stated outcome. Include positive, negative and recovery cases where relevant. Keep answer guidance and learner tasks consistent.

Verify product version, edition, events, APIs and quotas in the actual teaching environment. Mark pseudocode clearly. Explain assumptions and missing evidence. Use synthetic data unless a permission cleared example is supplied. Keep secrets and confidential identifiers out of assets.

For website copy, resolve public placeholders, substantiate experience and affiliations, confirm dates and prices, and use accurate credential wording. For changes, record affected assets, prior and new versions, the reviewer and active cohort impact. Require a human technical rehearsal before delivery.

The runner validates structure and permitted local paths. It cannot establish factual accuracy, teaching quality or business approval. Those checks remain with the assigned team reviewers.

## Deeper lesson checks
- Sample input and expected result are specific and internally consistent.
- Negative and recovery cases exercise the actual module concept.
- Independent assignment changes the scenario rather than repeating guided work.
- Trainer answers explain cause, not only restate an acceptance check.
- Live plus practice minutes match the catalog. Extra activities are optional or replace named activities.
- Platform claims cite supporting official material; future release notes are labeled planned.
- Local simulator tests are not claimed as live-model or Zoho tests.
- Observed results, estimates and not-run cases stay distinct.
