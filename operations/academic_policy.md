# Assessment and Academic Operations

## Practical rubric

Version 0.2 uses materials/Practical_Assessment_Rubric.md as the detailed marking reference. Apply the same practical dimensions to module labs and capstones, with scenario-specific acceptance checks. Award zero for absent evidence, half credit for partially correct evidence and full credit for demonstrated behavior. Record reasons for deductions and calibrate with two trainers before cohort marking.

| Dimension | Points | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business fit and traceability | 20 | Requirement, assumptions and design mapping |
| Correct behavior | 30 | Reproducible sample input, expected and actual output |
| Failure and recovery | 25 | Negative case, correction and successful retest |
| Evidence and handover | 15 | Environment, record IDs, observed results and support steps |
| Independent explanation | 10 | New input and oral defense |

For ZIM, ZDV and AAB, the proposed course grade combines quizzes 15 percent, module labs 35 percent, capstone 40 percent and defense 10 percent. Overall grade, module-lab average and capstone must each reach 70; attendance must reach 80 percent, critical gates must pass and the academy reviewer must accept the evidence. CAW combines pilot brief 70 percent and demonstration 30 percent, with both practical components reaching 70. Owner confirmation is required before enrollment.

Critical failures override the aggregate score. Examples are exposing a token, unauthorized access, unexplained destructive changes or duplicate business operations during a required replay test. Ask the learner to remediate and retest the affected evidence before deciding the result. A high average cannot compensate for these failures.

## Admission and baseline

Collect the learner goal, relevant experience, time availability, preferred language, access needs and only the contact information needed to deliver training. Run a 20 minute diagnostic with five concept questions and one practical task. Record initial skill levels; do not use an AI inferred personality or employability score. Offer a bridge lesson where the missing prerequisite is small; otherwise recommend preparatory study and a later admission practical.

## Delivery and review rhythm

Before a cohort opens, the academic lead verifies the syllabus, edition dependent features, synthetic lab datasets, answer keys and trainer environment. The operations owner confirms the schedule and publishes a roster with the agreed timezone. A proposed pilot capacity is 12 to 15 learners for practical cohorts; confirm this from trainer review capacity rather than treating it as a platform limit.

Each week, run the first live session as explanation and demonstration, and the second as guided build and review. Independent practice completes the module artifact. The trainer checks submissions before the next dependent module and records common errors. Offer a weekly office hour only if staffing is available; allocate its time and cost separately from the curriculum hours.

## Attendance and accommodations

Track attended instructional minutes divided by scheduled instructional minutes. Proposed eligibility is 80 percent attendance plus all required artifacts. An academic lead may approve a recorded lesson and equivalent practical makeup; record the exception and evidence. Provide readable notes, captions where available, downloadable instructions and an alternative demonstration format where needed. Confirm individual needs privately.

## Submission and AI use

The proposed due date is 48 hours after the final module session. Record late work and agree a recovery deadline. Learners may use approved AI help for explanation or debugging, but must disclose substantial assistance, verify outputs and explain their own work in the practical defense. Copying an answer without understanding does not meet the independence criterion. Do not put real client data into learner exercises.

## Retakes and appeals

Provide one remedial review and one equivalent retake within 14 days as a proposed policy. Confirm fees and further attempts before enrollment. Use a new scenario with the same competencies. The learner may request a second human review within seven days of the decision; a different qualified reviewer checks the rubric and evidence. AI can draft feedback but cannot finalize marks or appeal decisions.

## Credential and portfolio rules

Use the wording Wooplix Academy Certificate of Demonstrated Competence and state the program, level, learner name, award date, syllabus version and reviewer. Do not label it an official Zoho certification without a verified authorization. An attendance record is distinct from a competence certificate.

Assign a unique certificate identifier only after the human decision. Store the assessment record, rubric version and issuer approval. The future verification service should show certificate validity and revocation state, with limited learner information and explicit publication consent. The starter pack contains a specification and text template, not issued credentials or a public verification service.

Each portfolio contains a business problem, synthetic process diagram, selected screenshots, test evidence, a short demonstration and reflection. Remove personal information and client identifiers. Residency eligibility means ready for supervised internal work; it does not guarantee employment or a client assignment.

## Curriculum change procedure

Log the trigger, evidence and intended learner outcome. Examples include a product change, repeated lab failure, learner feedback or a corporate requirement. The agent drafts changed materials and an impact report. The trainer checks correctness and workload; the academic lead reviews prerequisites, assessments and credential impact. Publish a new version for future cohorts. Active cohorts retain their stated assessment rules unless a documented correction is necessary and learners receive notice.

Use versions such as 0.1 for an initial draft, 1.0 for the first approved syllabus, 1.1 for a minor correction, and 2.0 for a material outcome or assessment change. Store the previous version, reviewer, approval date and affected assets. The optional runner stages text and can copy individually reviewed artifacts into an approved folder; it does not automatically update the live LMS.
