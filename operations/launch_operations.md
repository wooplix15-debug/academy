# Launch and Business Operations

## Roles and responsibilities

The academy owner selects the offer, budget and launch date. The academic lead approves learning outcomes and assessment policy. Trainers verify labs and grade work. The operations owner manages rosters, schedules and attendance. The content manager maintains sources and versions. A technical owner builds integrations and access controls. An expert mentor owns implementation review. One person may hold several roles, but every approval must identify the responsible person.

## Scheduling procedure

Collect trainer availability, learner timezones, excluded dates, session lengths and room or video capacity. Expand the ordered modules into required sessions. Store timestamps in UTC and present them in the learner timezone. Check trainer overlap, learner overlap, capacity, holidays, prerequisites and deadline spacing before publishing. The initial owner display timezone is Asia/Kolkata; confirm cohort timezones separately.

For the three longer courses, schedule two two hour classes per module week with a suggested one day gap, then a two hour independent lab. For the workshop, deliver modules one and two on day one, modules three and four on day two, and the four independent hours over the next two weeks. Specific dates remain unset until availability is supplied. A canceled prerequisite session must be rescheduled before its dependent lab. Notify learners only after the operations owner confirms the revised schedule. The AI may propose dates; a deterministic service must validate them before any future calendar integration.

## Enrollment to completion procedure

Receive inquiry and record consent and interest. Send the approved course outline, prerequisites, price, attendance and retake terms. Review the diagnostic and recommend a track. Confirm payment or corporate assignment through the responsible business owner, then create learner access. Send account setup and orientation instructions. Deliver modules, record evidence, assess the practical, and route results for human signoff. Issue the correct completion record and request feedback and portfolio consent.

## Corporate training package

Use a discovery form to record the sponsor, employee roles, current tools, one process, pain points, approved data, success measures and constraints. Produce a proposal with scope, exclusions, attendee count, hours, trainer role, deliverables, assessment, schedule options and commercial terms supplied by the owner. Avoid filling missing terms with invented commitments.

Baseline and final practical tasks should test the same competency with different examples. Report employee completion only to authorized corporate reviewers. Provide aggregate attendance, submission and competency results, unresolved blockers and next training needs. A consulting discovery handoff needs sponsor agreement and separate scope. A workshop does not authorize production implementation.

## Knowledge and case study intake

Accept only sources the owner is entitled to use. Record document ID, title, owner, source, version, product, audience, sensitivity, permission, status and review date. A subject matter expert checks important technical content. Mark internal strategy separately from approved technical guidance. Sanitized project cases need client or internal permission before learner publication.

Keep client specific documents out of the general teaching library. The standalone agent runner accepts only explicitly approved local text files and sends those selected files to the configured model service. It has no tenant aware retrieval system. Client confidential content needs a separately reviewed deployment and access design before use.

## Commercial planning worksheet

Set prices after estimating delivery effort and testing demand. Per cohort contribution equals paid seats multiplied by net collected fee, minus trainer hours times trainer rate, review hours times reviewer rate, software allocation, payment costs, learner support, marketing allocation and refunds. Break even seats equal fixed cohort costs divided by positive contribution per additional seat, rounded up. If contribution per seat is zero or negative, change the offer before launch.

For corporate offers, cost discovery, customization, facilitation, employee reviews, reporting, travel if applicable and account management. Treat consulting revenue as a separate opportunity rather than assumed course profit. Currency, taxes, invoicing, refund language, recording consent and data retention need owner and qualified local review before publication.

## Measures for the first cohort

| Measure | Definition | Decision it supports |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enrollment conversion | Confirmed enrollments divided by qualified inquiries in the same period | Improve the offer or admission process |
| Practical completion | Learners submitting all required artifacts divided by enrolled learners | Adjust support and workload |
| Competency pass rate | Human confirmed practical passes divided by learners assessed | Review prerequisite fit and teaching |
| Attendance | Attended minutes divided by scheduled learner minutes | Identify delivery obstacles |
| Review turnaround | Time from submission to recorded feedback | Match intake to trainer capacity |
| Skill change | Final minus baseline score on comparable competency tasks | Check learning effectiveness |
| Cohort contribution | Net collections minus allocated delivery costs | Decide whether the offer is sustainable |
| Agent draft acceptance | Drafts accepted after review divided by drafts reviewed | Improve prompts and source material |

Record actual results before setting targets. An example pilot target may guide discussion but is not evidence of capacity or learning impact.

## Launch sequence

Weeks one and two are an academic preparation period: appoint owners, confirm the audience and schedule, verify licenses, review the Implementation Practitioner syllabus, rehearse its labs, approve terms and build the first lesson assets. Weeks three and four cover a small workshop and the practitioner orientation plus first two modules. Continue the ten week practitioner course and review evidence weekly. Refine the developer and AI materials while the pilot runs. These are relative planning periods, not a published calendar.

Use an existing approved training and operations stack if one is already available. Evaluate a Zoho centered stack and a custom platform against feature access, trainer workflows, privacy, integration effort and recurring cost. The source plan's Next.js, FastAPI and database choices are architecture options. Curriculum delivery can begin before a custom platform is finished.

The source document gives an 8 to 12 week focused MVP estimate, while its separate phase durations sum to 15 to 24 weeks if done sequentially. Parallel delivery or reduced scope would be required to reconcile those figures. Obtain a staffed estimate before committing to a software launch date.
