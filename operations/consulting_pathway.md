# Supervised Implementation and Consulting Pathway

This pathway connects the four training offers to scoped business work. It is not a fifth public course or a promise that every learner will receive client work.

First, a corporate sponsor or prospect agrees to a separate discovery discussion. A consultant documents the business problem, current process, data, users, success measures and missing decisions. The responsible expert reviews scope, commercial terms, access and delivery responsibilities before a proposal is sent.

Second, use assessment evidence to identify appropriate supervised contributions. A passed course practical is one input. The mentor must also review project specific skills, availability and access. Begin with internal or synthetic work. Live client involvement needs explicit assignment, supervision and any relevant confidentiality and client approvals.

Third, create milestones, acceptance tests and an escalation path. Learners can draft requirements, build reviewed components or prepare test evidence within the agreed role. The expert remains responsible for design, client commitments, final acceptance and release. Course completion does not grant production access.

Finally, close the project with a support handover and retrospective. Convert useful lessons into teaching material only after permission and sanitization review. Store actual evidence for any published outcome. Update related course modules and assessments through the team change process.

Required artifacts: discovery brief; scope and exclusions; responsibility matrix; permission record; project plan; design review; test log; expert signoff; handover; publication permission for any case study. Templates are in operations/templates.md.
