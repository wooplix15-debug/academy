## Course overview

Translate a business requirement into a tested CRM configuration and deliver a documented implementation handover. This program combines explanation, guided practice and a business scenario that you can use to demonstrate the skills you have learned.

## Who should join

Corporate CRM administrators, sales operations teams and implementation consultants.

## Before you join

Demonstrate CRM record, workflow and spreadsheet import proficiency in the admission practical; use a bridge lesson for a small gap.

## What you will build

Deliver a lead to delivery CRM implementation for fictional Nova Services. Your project evidence includes discovery brief, scope, process map, data model, permission matrix, automation register, migration reconciliation, UAT log and handover.

## Skills you will practice

- Discovery and scope
- Solution and data design
- Permissions and environment
- Workflow configuration
- Blueprint and approval design
- Data migration
- Integration planning
- Reporting and adoption
- UAT and release
- Implementation defense
