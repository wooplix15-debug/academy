# Corporate Process Baseline Worked Teaching Example

Module CAW M01

## Sponsor scenario

A fictional sales department handles 100 inquiries per week. Staff estimate that each inquiry takes eight minutes to summarize and route. These figures are workshop assumptions, not measured Wooplix results. Managers want to test whether a reviewed AI summary could reduce handling time without changing the routing decision.

## Facilitation sequence

Use ten minutes to agree the process and goal, 25 minutes to map intake through routing, 25 minutes to demonstrate a synthetic example, 40 minutes for team mapping and 20 minutes for review. Each group submits one process map with step owners, input and output fields, exception paths and unresolved questions.

## Worked baseline

The estimated current weekly task time is 100 multiplied by eight minutes, or 800 minutes. Do not claim savings from this estimate. Collect a small representative sample with observed preparation, review and correction time. If a trial averages five minutes including review, the sample suggests three minutes less per item under those conditions. Quality errors, rework, tooling cost and applicability still need measurement before proposing a business case.

## Follow through assignment

Within the one hour module practice budget, define a five case synthetic trial. Record handling time, summary accuracy, routing accuracy, corrections and reviewer decision. Do not use customer personal information. Bring the findings into the next module’s opportunity scorecard.

## Completion checks

The sponsor agrees the mapped process; every measure has a unit and source; estimates are marked; the pilot has an owner; and incorrect summaries cannot cause automatic routing. A good workshop result can be a decision that the process needs better data before AI is useful.

## Discussion prompts

Which fields are necessary to route an inquiry? What makes a summary wrong? Who reviews exceptions? How will the team know whether the trial improved the process? What would cause the sponsor to stop the pilot?
