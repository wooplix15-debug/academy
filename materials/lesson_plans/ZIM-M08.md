# ZIM-M08 Reporting and adoption
Version 0.2 | Team review required | Synthetic teaching case

## Purpose
- Define business metrics correctly
- Plan end user adoption

## Prerequisite and capstone contribution
Use the previous module evidence as input: ZIM-M07

Save this module evidence in the same course portfolio: Metric definitions and training outline.

## Explain the concept
A useful metric has a population, numerator, denominator, time window and exclusions. Adoption evidence comes from users completing tasks, not attendance alone.

## Demonstration and sample input
Synthetic weekly cohort: 20 qualified leads and 5 converted leads gives 25 percent cohort conversion. Open pipeline INR 120000 plus INR 80000 equals INR 200000; closed or cancelled deals are excluded.

## Live teaching sequence
- Retrieve prior learning: 15 minutes
- Explain and predict: 30 minutes
- Trainer demonstration: 35 minutes
- Guided build: 75 minutes
- Break: 15 minutes
- Failure and recovery tests: 40 minutes
- Evidence review and exit check: 30 minutes

## Guided lab
1. Define conversion and open-pipeline metrics.
2. Calculate the examples by hand.
3. Reproduce them in a report using verified filters.
4. Ask a learner to create, qualify and hand off a lead without narration.
5. Record errors and support owner.

## Expected result
The report reproduces 25 percent and INR 200000 for the supplied example. Numerator belongs to the same lead cohort as denominator; currency is shown.

## Negative test
Mix conversions from old leads with only this week new leads. Identify why the resulting rate is invalid.

## Recovery test
Correct the date filters, recalculate and annotate the previous dashboard error.

## Independent practice
Define time-to-first-response including business hours and pending exceptions.

Budget: 120 minutes. Use 15 minutes planning, 75 building and testing, 30 documenting.

## Submit and assess
Metric definitions and training outline.
- Filters and denominators are explicit
- Users can complete core tasks
- Support ownership is named
