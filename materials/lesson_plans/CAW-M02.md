# CAW-M02 AI assisted work
Version 0.2 | Team review required | Synthetic teaching case

## Purpose
- Test an assisted task
- Identify where review is required

## Prerequisite and capstone contribution
Use the previous module evidence as input: CAW-M01

Save this module evidence in the same course portfolio: Before and after task example.

## Explain the concept
An assisted output is checked against its input before use. A well-written message can still invent an agreement, commitment or customer fact.

## Demonstration and sample input
Synthetic input: customer asked for a demo next week; no date or discount agreed. A good draft proposes scheduling. A bad draft confirms Tuesday and a 20 percent discount.

## Live teaching sequence
- Baseline and recap: 10 minutes
- Explain and predict: 15 minutes
- Facilitator demonstration: 20 minutes
- Team exercise: 40 minutes
- Break: 10 minutes
- Review and decision: 25 minutes

## Guided lab
1. Write a prompt using the approved facts.
2. Generate or use supplied contrasting drafts.
3. Highlight every factual assertion.
4. Check assertions against input.
5. Correct unsupported commitments.
6. Time generation plus review.

## Expected result
Reviewed draft has no invented appointment or discount. Before-and-after timing includes correction work.

## Negative test
Insert a confidential customer note. Use a sanitized substitute unless the chosen tool and permissions are approved for it.

## Recovery test
Reject the bad draft and regenerate using explicit missing-date behavior.

## Independent practice
Draft a support summary that distinguishes customer statements from confirmed findings.

Budget: 60 minutes. Use 10 minutes planning, 35 practicing and 15 documenting.

## Submit and assess
Before and after task example.
- Output is checked against input
- Confidential data is excluded
- Reviewer ownership is clear

## Learner reflection
What should the draft do when a meeting date is unknown?

Describe one failed input, your correction and evidence that the correction worked.
