# ZDV-M12 Trainer answer guide
## Expected demonstration
Learner independently explains ownership and replay decisions. Peer completes reconciliation with no secrets in the bundle.
## Oral answer
Question: What distinguishes a defensible portfolio from a screenshot collection?

It contains requirements, design choices, code behavior, reproducible tests and a clear statement of what was actually run.
## Failure diagnosis
The reviewer asks whether local SQLite success proves live CRM race behavior. The answer must state the simulation boundary.

Do not accept a happy-path screenshot as evidence for this case. Require its exact input, observed result and explanation.
## Recovery explanation
Repair one failed support step and repeat the peer exercise.
## Common misconception
Ask the learner to distinguish the business rule from the feature used to implement it, and a simulated result from a connected-product result.
## Independent task review
Create a client-neutral portfolio explanation with sanitized screenshots and limitations.

Check that the learner adapts the rule, preserves prior evidence and identifies affected tests. Several designs can pass; accept alternatives only with testable assumptions and evidence.
## Scoring
Apply the shared practical rubric. A critical access or unauthorized-write failure requires repair and reassessment, even when total points are at least 70.
