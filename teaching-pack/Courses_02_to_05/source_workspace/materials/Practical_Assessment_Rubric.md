# Practical assessment rubric
Proposed academy policy for owner confirmation. Score each module practical out of 100.
- Business fit and traceability 20: correct requirement, assumptions and design mapping.
- Correct behavior 30: reproducible demonstration with sample input and expected output.
- Failure and recovery 25: negative case, safe correction and retest.
- Evidence and handover 15: clear environment, IDs, observed results and support steps.
- Independent explanation 10: new input and oral defense without following a memorized script.
Use 0 for absent evidence, half credit for partially correct evidence and full credit for demonstrated behavior. Record the reason for every deduction. A blank actual result is not a pass.

For ZIM, ZDV and AAB, course grade is quizzes 15 percent, module labs 35 percent, capstone 40 percent and practical defense 10 percent. Example: scores 80, 75, 85 and 70 yield 79.75 overall. Module-lab average and capstone must each reach 70 under this proposed interpretation of the practical minimum. Overall must reach 70, attendance at least 80 percent, and critical gates pass. Certificate decisions require the academy reviewer. No official Zoho credential is implied.

CAW uses pilot brief 70 percent and demonstration 30 percent; both practical components must reach 70 with the same critical gate and reviewer decision.

Critical gate examples: restricted-data exposure, unauthorized write, embedded credentials, or unintended destructive effect. Repair, document and reassess before passing. A high numeric average cannot waive the gate.

Calibration: two trainers independently score one strong, one borderline and one failed portfolio. Compare deductions and agree interpretations before marking the cohort. Allow one scoped resubmission with repaired evidence and an independent retest; the owner sets the time window before enrollment.
