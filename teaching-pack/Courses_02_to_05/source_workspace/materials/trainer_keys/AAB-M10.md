# AAB-M10 Trainer answer guide
## Expected demonstration
A complete 20-case register with honest execution status, a bounded draft workflow and maintenance owner. Wooplix assessment does not become an official vendor credential.
## Oral answer
Question: What must accompany an agent handover?

Tool boundaries, source permissions, evaluation evidence, cost controls, operator recovery and review ownership.
## Failure diagnosis
The reviewer asks whether passing mocks proves production readiness. Explain the remaining model, authorization and connected-tool tests.

Do not accept a happy-path screenshot as evidence for this case. Require its exact input, observed result and explanation.
## Recovery explanation
Withdraw a stale source and demonstrate that the next run omits it.
## Common misconception
Ask the learner to distinguish the business rule from the feature used to implement it, and a simulated result from a connected-product result.
## Independent task review
Prepare a department-specific assistant using approved source material and a new evaluation set.

Check that the learner adapts the rule, preserves prior evidence and identifies affected tests. Several designs can pass; accept alternatives only with testable assumptions and evidence.
## Scoring
Apply the shared practical rubric. A critical access or unauthorized-write failure requires repair and reassessment, even when total points are at least 70.
