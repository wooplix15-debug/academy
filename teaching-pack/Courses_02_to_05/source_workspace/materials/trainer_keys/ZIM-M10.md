# ZIM-M10 Trainer answer guide
## Expected demonstration
Reviewer can trace every requirement to design, configuration and test evidence. The learner can explain the change and limits independently.
## Oral answer
Question: What proves the handover is usable?

Another person performs an agreed operation or recovery task using the supplied documentation and records the result.
## Failure diagnosis
A reviewer asks for production deployment or an official Zoho certificate. Record these as outside the training exercise.

Do not accept a happy-path screenshot as evidence for this case. Require its exact input, observed result and explanation.
## Recovery explanation
The peer cannot find the failed-rule owner. Repair the runbook and repeat the handover test.
## Common misconception
Ask the learner to distinguish the business rule from the feature used to implement it, and a simulated result from a connected-product result.
## Independent task review
Prepare a ten-minute department-specific demonstration using the same implementation.

Check that the learner adapts the rule, preserves prior evidence and identifies affected tests. Several designs can pass; accept alternatives only with testable assumptions and evidence.
## Scoring
Apply the shared practical rubric. A critical access or unauthorized-write failure requires repair and reassessment, even when total points are at least 70.
