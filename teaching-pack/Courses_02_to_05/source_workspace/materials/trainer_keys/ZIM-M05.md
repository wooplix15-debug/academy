# ZIM-M05 Trainer answer guide
## Expected demonstration
A quote requiring approval cannot be sent through the normal route. Rejected quotes return for correction with their history intact. Record the edition and exact feature used.
## Oral answer
Question: Does a Blueprint automatically implement every approval requirement?

No. Map the requirement to the supported transition, approval or custom behavior and verify it in the training edition.
## Failure diagnosis
Attempt Draft to Sent for INR 50001 with no approval. Expected outcome is a blocked transition under the proposed policy.

Do not accept a happy-path screenshot as evidence for this case. Require its exact input, observed result and explanation.
## Recovery explanation
Reject then revise the amount and resubmit. Record whether prior approval is invalidated by the policy.
## Common misconception
Ask the learner to distinguish the business rule from the feature used to implement it, and a simulated result from a connected-product result.
## Independent task review
Add a discount-above-15-percent review rule and write all boundary tests before configuration.

Check that the learner adapts the rule, preserves prior evidence and identifies affected tests. Several designs can pass; accept alternatives only with testable assumptions and evidence.
## Scoring
Apply the shared practical rubric. A critical access or unauthorized-write failure requires repair and reassessment, even when total points are at least 70.
