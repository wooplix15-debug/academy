# CAW-M01 Business baseline
Version 0.2 | Team review required | Synthetic teaching case

## Purpose
- Map a current departmental process
- Identify measurable delays

## Prerequisite and capstone contribution
Start the course capstone evidence folder and record the training environment.

Save this module evidence in the same course portfolio: Current process and measured or explicitly estimated baseline.

## Explain the concept
A process baseline describes who performs each step, how long active work takes and where waiting occurs. Distinguish observed timing from estimates.

## Demonstration and sample input
Synthetic follow-up tasks take 8, 10 and 12 active minutes: mean 10 minutes per task. At 100 tasks weekly, baseline active effort is 1000 minutes or 16.67 hours; this is a teaching example.

## Live teaching sequence
- Baseline and recap: 10 minutes
- Explain and predict: 15 minutes
- Facilitator demonstration: 20 minutes
- Team exercise: 40 minutes
- Break: 10 minutes
- Review and decision: 25 minutes

## Guided lab
1. Agree the process boundary with the sponsor.
2. Draw trigger, roles, handoffs and completion.
3. Time three sanitized examples.
4. Record active and waiting time separately.
5. Note variation and exceptions.

## Expected result
A sponsor-reviewed process map and baseline sheet with units, sample size and observed or estimated labels.

## Negative test
A participant claims the task always takes ten minutes from one example. Record the evidence limitation.

## Recovery test
Repeat timing across a different case type and revise the baseline range.

## Independent practice
Map employee onboarding using the same timing sheet and a different role set.

Budget: 60 minutes. Use 10 minutes planning, 35 practicing and 15 documenting.

## Submit and assess
Current process and measured or explicitly estimated baseline.
- Sponsor agrees the process
- Measures have units
- Estimates are labeled

## Learner reflection
Why separate waiting from active time?

Describe one failed input, your correction and evidence that the correction worked.

## Source verification
Official reference IDs: S01, S08. Use approved synthetic examples; label estimates and any tool behavior that has not been verified. Examples and thresholds are proposed lab rules.

## Trainer preparation
Use the separate trainer key. Rehearse the positive, negative and recovery case. Provide a mock equivalent if required product access is unavailable; label the evidence accordingly.
