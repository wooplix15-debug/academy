# CAW-M03 Automation opportunity design
Version 0.2 | Team review required | Synthetic teaching case

## Purpose
- Prioritize feasible opportunities
- Explain exception handling

## Prerequisite and capstone contribution
Use the previous module evidence as input: CAW-M02

Save this module evidence in the same course portfolio: Opportunity scorecard and pilot workflow.

## Explain the concept
Prioritization combines value, readiness and failure consequence. A score guides discussion but cannot remove a missing permission or mandatory dependency.

## Demonstration and sample input
Proposed formula: value plus readiness minus risk, each 1 to 5. Follow-up drafting 4+5-2=7; automatic refunds 5+2-5=2; support summaries 3+4-2=5. Drafting is the first discussion candidate.

## Live teaching sequence
- Baseline and recap: 10 minutes
- Explain and predict: 15 minutes
- Facilitator demonstration: 20 minutes
- Team exercise: 40 minutes
- Break: 10 minutes
- Review and decision: 25 minutes

## Guided lab
1. List three opportunities.
2. Score each dimension and justify it.
3. Identify tool, data and access dependencies.
4. Apply any blocking conditions.
5. Draw the highest feasible pilot with exception and review paths.

## Expected result
A scorecard with rationale and a bounded pilot. The highest score is not an authorization to spend, publish or access client systems.

## Negative test
A high-scoring idea needs unavailable customer data. Mark it blocked until the dependency is resolved.

## Recovery test
Rescope to approved synthetic data and review whether the pilot still tests the intended value.

## Independent practice
Change the risk weights and explain how the ranking changes.

Budget: 60 minutes. Use 10 minutes planning, 35 practicing and 15 documenting.

## Submit and assess
Opportunity scorecard and pilot workflow.
- Dependencies are named
- Failure paths exist
- No unverified savings claim is presented

## Learner reflection
Why record the rationale beside a score?

Describe one failed input, your correction and evidence that the correction worked.
