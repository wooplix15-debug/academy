# ZIM-M10 Implementation defense
Version 0.2 | Team review required | Synthetic teaching case

## Purpose
- Demonstrate business fit and configuration
- Complete a delivery handover

## Prerequisite and capstone contribution
Use the previous module evidence as input: ZIM-M09

Save this module evidence in the same course portfolio: Capstone bundle and reviewer feedback.

## Explain the concept
A handover transfers the ability to operate, diagnose and recover the implementation. The practical defense checks learner understanding beyond a prepared demonstration.

## Demonstration and sample input
Give a second trainer only the configuration register and runbook. Ask them to locate R01, explain its routing, diagnose a missing region and find recovery steps without help.

## Live teaching sequence
- Retrieve prior learning: 15 minutes
- Explain and predict: 30 minutes
- Trainer demonstration: 35 minutes
- Guided build: 75 minutes
- Break: 15 minutes
- Failure and recovery tests: 40 minutes
- Evidence review and exit check: 30 minutes

## Guided lab
1. Assemble prior module evidence into one indexed bundle.
2. Demonstrate eight UAT cases.
3. Explain one configuration tradeoff.
4. Receive a surprise region-rule change.
5. Update the change record and show impacted tests.
6. Ask a peer to execute the runbook.

## Expected result
Reviewer can trace every requirement to design, configuration and test evidence. The learner can explain the change and limits independently.

## Negative test
A reviewer asks for production deployment or an official Zoho certificate. Record these as outside the training exercise.

## Recovery test
The peer cannot find the failed-rule owner. Repair the runbook and repeat the handover test.

## Independent practice
Prepare a ten-minute department-specific demonstration using the same implementation.

Budget: 120 minutes. Use 15 minutes planning, 75 building and testing, 30 documenting.

## Submit and assess
Capstone bundle and reviewer feedback.
- Eight acceptance scenarios pass
- Known limitations are disclosed
- Another trainer can follow the handover

## Learner reflection
What proves the handover is usable?

Describe one failed input, your correction and evidence that the correction worked.

## Source verification

## Trainer preparation
Use the separate trainer key. Rehearse the positive, negative and recovery case. Provide a mock equivalent if required product access is unavailable; label the evidence accordingly.
