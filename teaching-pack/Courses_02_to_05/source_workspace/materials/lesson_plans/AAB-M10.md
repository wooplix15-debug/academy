# AAB-M10 Builder demonstration and defense
Version 0.2 | Team review required | Synthetic teaching case

## Purpose
- Demonstrate success and safe failure
- Explain limitations and maintenance

## Prerequisite and capstone contribution
Use the previous module evidence as input: AAB-M09

Save this module evidence in the same course portfolio: Final assistant bundle and reviewer score.

## Explain the concept
A builder demonstration shows both supported output and correct failure. Maintenance ownership includes source refresh, evaluation drift and review of content changes.

## Demonstration and sample input
Show a cited lesson, missing-source abstention, restricted-source denial, instruction attack, malformed output, tool failure, duplicate proposal, stale version, cost stop and certificate refusal.

## Live teaching sequence
- Retrieve prior learning: 15 minutes
- Explain and predict: 30 minutes
- Trainer demonstration: 35 minutes
- Guided build: 75 minutes
- Break: 15 minutes
- Failure and recovery tests: 40 minutes
- Evidence review and exit check: 30 minutes

## Guided lab
1. Demonstrate ten scenarios.
2. Explain remaining evaluation cases.
3. Show actual versus not-run labels.
4. Perform one repair.
5. Give a second trainer the source-update runbook.
6. Record remaining limits and reviewer decision.

## Expected result
A complete 20-case register with honest execution status, a bounded draft workflow and maintenance owner. Wooplix assessment does not become an official vendor credential.

## Negative test
The reviewer asks whether passing mocks proves production readiness. Explain the remaining model, authorization and connected-tool tests.

## Recovery test
Withdraw a stale source and demonstrate that the next run omits it.

## Independent practice
Prepare a department-specific assistant using approved source material and a new evaluation set.

Budget: 120 minutes. Use 15 minutes planning, 75 building and testing, 30 documenting.

## Submit and assess
Final assistant bundle and reviewer score.
- Twenty evaluation cases are recorded
- Critical permission tests all pass
- Human reviewer owns final acceptance

## Learner reflection
What must accompany an agent handover?

Describe one failed input, your correction and evidence that the correction worked.

## Source verification
Official reference IDs: S07, S08. Check model, retrieval and tool behavior in the approved environment. Examples and thresholds are proposed lab rules.
