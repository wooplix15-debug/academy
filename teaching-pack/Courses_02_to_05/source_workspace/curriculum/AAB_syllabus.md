# AI and Agentic Business Automation Builder

Program AAB | Applied Builder | 10 weeks | 60 total hours

Audience: Zoho developers, automation practitioners and technical corporate teams.

Prerequisites: Basic business process and spreadsheet skills. A Python or JavaScript bridge is required for the coded track; a supervised workflow track can use no code tools.

Completion outcome: Build an evaluated knowledge assistant and a bounded draft generation workflow for a business use case.

Environment: Approved model access, sanitized knowledge documents, a workflow tool or coding environment, mock business APIs and an evaluation dataset.

Time allocation: 40 facilitated hours and 20 independent practical hours. Quiz and review time are included in those hours.

## Module syllabus

### Week 1 AI use cases and limits

Module AAB-M01 | 4 facilitated hours | 2 practice hours

Learners will select a measurable automation use case; distinguish generation from deterministic execution.

Syllabus: Language model basics; Business task analysis; Accuracy and verification.

Practical: Compare three academy use cases and select one with an observable success measure.

Submission: Use case scorecard and baseline.

Assessment checks: Task has a clear user; Failure consequence is stated; Baseline work can be measured.

### Week 2 Prompt design and structured output

Module AAB-M02 | 4 facilitated hours | 2 practice hours

Learners will write an input and output contract; test a prompt against examples.

Syllabus: Task context and examples; Schemas; Missing information behavior.

Practical: Generate a lesson outline from approved topics with required fields.

Submission: Prompt, schema and five trial outputs.

Assessment checks: Required fields appear; Assumptions are labeled; Unsupported details are marked for review.

### Week 3 Business workflows

Module AAB-M03 | 4 facilitated hours | 2 practice hours

Learners will separate proposal from execution; map exception and review paths.

Syllabus: Triggers and stages; Human review; Deterministic checks.

Practical: Design an assignment draft workflow with a trainer review step.

Submission: Workflow diagram and decision rules.

Assessment checks: Each stage has an owner; Failures have a recovery path; Publication is a separate action.

### Week 4 Knowledge preparation

Module AAB-M04 | 4 facilitated hours | 2 practice hours

Learners will classify usable source material; keep source and permission metadata.

Syllabus: Cleaning and sanitization; Chunking; Versions and access labels.

Practical: Convert three synthetic academy notes into indexed knowledge records.

Submission: Knowledge manifest and cleaned records.

Assessment checks: Each record has an owner and date; Confidential content is excluded; Draft and approved sources are distinguished.

### Week 5 Retrieval and cited answers

Module AAB-M05 | 4 facilitated hours | 2 practice hours

Learners will retrieve relevant context; explain when the assistant should abstain.

Syllabus: Search and embeddings concepts; RAG; Citations and relevance.

Practical: Answer five questions using only the supplied knowledge set.

Submission: Answers with source identifiers and retrieval notes.

Assessment checks: Citations support the answer; Missing evidence causes abstention; Conflicting sources are surfaced.

### Week 6 Tools and bounded agents

Module AAB-M06 | 4 facilitated hours | 2 practice hours

Learners will define an allowed tool contract; constrain action proposals.

Syllabus: Tool inputs and outputs; Planning and state; Allowlist and permissions.

Practical: Implement or simulate course lookup and draft lesson tools.

Submission: Tool contracts and workflow traces.

Assessment checks: Inputs are validated; Unauthorized requests are denied; No unrestricted execution tool is exposed.

### Week 7 Evaluation and adversarial cases

Module AAB-M07 | 4 facilitated hours | 2 practice hours

Learners will design realistic evaluation cases; measure task success and failure.

Syllabus: Golden examples; Injection attempts; Human and automated evaluation.

Practical: Build a 20 case evaluation set including five failure or adversarial cases.

Submission: Evaluation CSV and result report.

Assessment checks: Cases include missing sources; Permission violations are counted separately; Scoring rules are reproducible.

### Week 8 Operations and cost

Module AAB-M08 | 4 facilitated hours | 2 practice hours

Learners will measure usage and latency; plan bounded retries and cost limits.

Syllabus: Tokens and request logging; Timeouts and retry policy; Quality versus cost.

Practical: Measure a small controlled workflow and design a per run budget.

Submission: Usage log and operating limits.

Assessment checks: Observed values are separated from estimates; Retries are bounded; Logs avoid sensitive content.

### Week 9 Capstone workflow build

Module AAB-M09 | 4 facilitated hours | 2 practice hours

Learners will connect approved knowledge and generation; stage a reviewable update.

Syllabus: Draft generation; Validation; Version history.

Practical: Build the Academy assistant and test a curriculum update request.

Submission: Draft artifact, change report and evaluation results.

Assessment checks: Update preserves existing evidence; Changed files are identified; No automatic credential or external write occurs.

### Week 10 Builder demonstration and defense

Module AAB-M10 | 4 facilitated hours | 2 practice hours

Learners will demonstrate success and safe failure; explain limitations and maintenance.

Syllabus: Evaluation review; Practical defense; Support handover.

Practical: Demonstrate ten capstone scenarios and explain five remaining evaluation cases.

Submission: Final assistant bundle and reviewer score.

Assessment checks: Twenty evaluation cases are recorded; Critical permission tests all pass; Human reviewer owns final acceptance.

## Capstone and assessment

Build a Wooplix style Academy assistant that drafts a cited lesson or knowledge answer and proposes a reviewed change.

Required evidence: Use case brief, knowledge manifest, retrieval design, prompt and schema, draft workflow, 20 evaluation cases, cost log, permission design and demonstration.

Scenario tests: Supported question; missing source; stale source; conflicting source; unauthorized document; malicious instruction in a document; invalid output; tool failure; duplicate action proposal; request to issue a certificate.

Assessment weighting: quizzes 15 percent, module labs 35 percent, capstone 40 percent, practical defense 10 percent.

Proposed version 0.2 pass rules: overall score, module-lab average and capstone each at least 70 percent; at least 80 percent attendance with approved makeups; every required submission; all critical permission and data integrity checks passed; and acceptance by a named human reviewer. Practical defense contributes 10 percent to the overall score. Use materials/Practical_Assessment_Rubric.md and confirm the policy before enrollment.
