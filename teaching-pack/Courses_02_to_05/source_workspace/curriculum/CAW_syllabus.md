# Corporate AI and Automation Discovery Workshop

Program CAW | Workshop | 2 weeks | 12 total hours

Audience: Department leaders and employee teams with one business process to improve.

Prerequisites: Sponsor identifies one process, attendees, available tools and approved synthetic examples before the workshop.

Completion outcome: Produce a prioritized automation opportunity and a reviewed small pilot brief.

Environment: Facilitator demonstration account, process worksheet and synthetic examples; attendees do not need production admin access.

Time allocation: 8 facilitated hours and 4 independent practical hours. Quiz and review time are included in those hours.

## Module syllabus

### Module 1 Business baseline

Module CAW-M01 | 2 facilitated hours | 1 practice hours

Learners will map a current departmental process; identify measurable delays.

Syllabus: Process walkthrough; Role mapping; Baseline measures.

Practical: Map lead follow up, support triage or employee onboarding.

Submission: Current process and measured or explicitly estimated baseline.

Assessment checks: Sponsor agrees the process; Measures have units; Estimates are labeled.

### Module 2 AI assisted work

Module CAW-M02 | 2 facilitated hours | 1 practice hours

Learners will test an assisted task; identify where review is required.

Syllabus: Prompt examples; Output verification; Sensitive data handling.

Practical: Use synthetic inputs to draft a follow up or support summary.

Submission: Before and after task example.

Assessment checks: Output is checked against input; Confidential data is excluded; Reviewer ownership is clear.

### Module 3 Automation opportunity design

Module CAW-M03 | 2 facilitated hours | 1 practice hours

Learners will prioritize feasible opportunities; explain exception handling.

Syllabus: Value and feasibility; Workflow design; Tool and permission needs.

Practical: Rank three opportunities and diagram one small pilot.

Submission: Opportunity scorecard and pilot workflow.

Assessment checks: Dependencies are named; Failure paths exist; No unverified savings claim is presented.

### Module 4 Pilot and follow through

Module CAW-M04 | 2 facilitated hours | 1 practice hours

Learners will define pilot acceptance and ownership; prepare a discovery handoff.

Syllabus: Pilot scope; Success measures; Next decision.

Practical: Finalize a two week pilot brief in this module's one independent hour. The program has four total independent hours distributed across its four modules.

Submission: Reviewed pilot brief.

Assessment checks: Scope is bounded; Owner and review date exist; Consulting discussion requires sponsor agreement.

## Capstone and assessment

Select one department process and define a small automation pilot.

Required evidence: Baseline process, opportunity scorecard, sample assisted output and pilot brief with owner and acceptance measures.

Scenario tests: Missing input; incorrect generated answer; unsupported action; reviewer rejection.

Assessment weighting: pilot brief 70 percent, demonstration 30 percent.

Proposed version 0.2 competence criteria: pilot brief and demonstration each at least 70 percent, critical checks passed and human reviewer acceptance. Confirm attendance and reassessment terms before enrollment.

Issue an attendance or completion record when the workshop deliverables are reviewed. This workshop does not award a practitioner credential.
